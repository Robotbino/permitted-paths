Does each step use the same model and tools?YesNo. Some steps use a small model, some a large model.
Are some steps independent?NoYes, and they can operate at the same time
Who makes the decision that the result is good?A check in the loop, for example the testsA node that did not write the result
What must occur after a failure of one step?Try againIsolate the failure. The other work continues.
Who must read the record of the path?Only your teamA person outside the team must see which step occurred

Three good causes for a new node: specialization, parallel work and independent verification. Do not use a graph if the task is open and you cannot give its steps before the run. If all the steps are code, a script is sufficient.
