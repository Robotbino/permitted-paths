Part 2 · control 1

In a loop, the model selects its own path. In a graph, you select the permitted paths.

The model reasons in a node, or it selects one permitted path.

And the sentence that follows from it: an instruction in a prompt is a suggestion. A node with an edge is a constraint.

This is the hinge of the whole course. A prompt is advice the model may follow. An edge is the only way out of a node. The refund bug at the start was a suggestion that the model was free to ignore.
