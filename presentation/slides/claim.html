The claim of the course

Put the structure outside the model. Then the model makes a smaller number of decisions, and the system makes the other decisions.

Graph Engineering is the design of the graph in which an agent system operates.

This is the whole course in two sentences. Everything after this slide is either evidence for it or a method of doing it. Note what it does not say: it does not say the model gets better, and it does not say you can make the model deterministic.
