Part 5 · what the system knows

All teams can buy the same models. The model is not the difference.

Control plane

Which steps are permitted, and in which sequence.

the permitted next step

Data plane

The agent runtime: models, tools and the calls they make.

a trace of each step

facts, types and rules

Intelligence plane

The ontology, the knowledge graph and the traces.

The difference between two agent systems is what each system knows about its own domain. That knowledge lives in a layer of its own.

Read the arrows. The control plane gives the path. The data plane does the work and writes a trace. The intelligence plane keeps the traces and gives back the facts, the types and the rules that the three controls use.

Teaching model. The word “plane” comes from network engineering. The three planes are a way to sort the parts of a design. They are not a standard. The talk gives two planes; the notes add the data plane as a name for the agent runtime.
