Part 8 · give it back

Can you give the ideas back without help?

1

Four questions

Answer each in your own words: why does the output change, what does a graph remove, what does an ontology add, what does a gate examine?

2

Four failures

Give the control that each failure needed. If two controls are applicable, say which one you make first, and why.

3

Your run as a context graph

Draw this session as a trace: the steps, what each step read, and where a check stops a wrong answer.

4

Your one page

One page for one system of your own: the nodes, the permitted results, and the check before each change.

In part 0, the full system was one drawing. In part 8, six arrows have no label, and you put each label on its arrow.
