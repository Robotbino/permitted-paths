The tool computes

A refund record is built and returned. Nothing is written: the agent is pure during the loop.

Gate 2 — FAIL

Rule LV-1: this order already has a refund. The transaction rolls back and the cause goes to the model.

The database

And if gate 2 ever has a bug, the unique constraint refuses the insert anyway.

One rule, said three times, in three places that the model cannot talk its way past. The prompt sentence said it once, in the one place that the model was free to ignore.
